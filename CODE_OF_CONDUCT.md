# Code of conduct

taskmill follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and patient. Many people here write in their second language.
- Discuss ideas and code, never the person behind them.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or images, or sharing anyone's
  private information.

It applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, and discussions, and
wherever you speak for the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, open the **⋯** menu of their comment, issue,
pull request, or discussion and choose **Report content**. You can send the
report to the maintainers of this repository or to GitHub; either way, only
they see it, not the person you report. Security issues go through the
[security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and act
as the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe: from a private correction, through a warning and a temporary ban,
to a permanent ban. They may hide or edit comments, lock conversations, and
block accounts.
